# Programme Fit and Current Requirements

Use this reference for application requirements, Why School, courses, faculty, laboratories, facilities, or values.

## Source priority

For current claims, use authoritative sources in this order:

1. programme admissions and curriculum pages;
2. official university or department catalogues;
3. official faculty and research-group pages;
4. official regulations or application portals.

Use blogs, forums, and social media only for perspective, never as the sole source for binding requirements.

## Verify time-sensitive information

Check the relevant intake year where possible. Distinguish:

- a course existing in the catalogue;
- the programme permitting students to take it;
- the course being scheduled for the applicant's future year.

If the future timetable is unavailable, say so. Use cautious language such as `the current curriculum includes` or `offerings such as`; do not promise enrolment.

Apply the same caution to faculty affiliation, research projects, deadlines, tuition, tests, and document requirements.

## Write fit as a relationship

For each proposed resource, establish:

- the applicant's demonstrated starting point;
- the specific gap or next question;
- what the resource offers;
- how the applicant intends to use it.

One or two well-connected resources are usually stronger than a catalogue.

Avoid generic praise, rankings, copied descriptions, slogans, and values that do not connect naturally to the applicant's evidence.

## Faculty mentions

Mention faculty only when their current work is genuinely relevant and the programme provides a realistic way to engage with it. Do not imply supervision, laboratory access, or collaboration without evidence.

## Reporting

Cite official sources when giving the user current factual guidance. Keep citations out of the statement unless the application format calls for them.
